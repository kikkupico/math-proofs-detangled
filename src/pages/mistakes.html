A checklist

Before you call it done

Direction

Does the proof start from what you know and end at the goal, not the other way round?

Names

Does every ∃ given get its own new letter?

Generality

For a “for all” goal, is the object arbitrary, not an example?

Statement

Did you prove the claim, not its converse?

Division

Is everything you divide by, or cancel, nonzero?

Every case

Do the cases, or the induction step, cover every value, including the smallest?

  • The “all horses are the same colour” paradox is usually credited to George Pólya. The 2 = 1 “proof” is a classic of unknown origin.
